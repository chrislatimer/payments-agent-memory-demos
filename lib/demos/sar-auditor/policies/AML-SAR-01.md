## 3 Investigation and disposition

Alerts are generated by Sentinel (transaction monitoring) and are assigned through the
Financial Crime Case Manager ("FCCM"). Risk tiers are assigned by Sentinel at generation
in accordance with Appendix B.

| Risk tier | Assignment SLA | Disposition SLA |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | 1 business day | 10 business days |
| Tier 2 | 2 business days | 20 business days |
| Tier 3 | 5 business days | 30 business days |

### 3.1 Case set-up

**3.1.1** The Investigator shall record in the FCCM case file the alert reference, the
alert typology assigned by Sentinel, and the transaction detail for the full review period
identified on the alert.

**3.1.2** Where the activity described by the Sentinel typology is not present in the
transaction detail, the Investigator shall record this in the case file and refer the
alert to the Tuning and Optimisation team under AML-SAR-01 §5.4.

### 3.2 Customer review

**3.2.1** The Investigator shall record the customer's current risk rating and the date of
their most recent due diligence review, as held in the CDD record maintained under
AML-KYC-02.

**3.2.2** The Investigator shall record the customer's expected activity as stated in the
CDD record, including expected monthly values and expected counterparty jurisdictions.

**3.2.3** The case file shall record the observed activity for the review period against
the expected activity at 3.2.2, stating the variance in value and in counterparty count.

### 3.3 Screening

**3.3.1** The Investigator shall screen the customer under AML-SCR-04 and record the
screening reference returned by Prism in the case file.

**3.3.2** The Investigator shall screen each counterparty appearing in the transaction
detail at 3.1.1 under AML-SCR-04 and record each screening reference in the case file.
Counterparties are not sampled.

### 3.4 Previous filings

**3.4.1** The Investigator shall search the SAR Register for filings and alerts raised in
respect of the customer in the preceding 24 months.

The SAR Register is indexed by the name recorded at the time of filing. Customer names
change and customers are frequently recorded under more than one name. The Investigator
shall therefore run the search against each name and identifier held against the customer
in the CDD record, including former names, trading names and registration numbers, and
shall record each search run and its result in the case file.

**3.4.2** Where a filing or alert is returned at 3.4.1, the Investigator shall refer the
case to the MLRO under AML-ESC-01 §2.1. The case may not be dispositioned by the
Investigator.

### 3.5 Source of funds

**3.5.1** Where credits in the transaction detail exceed USD 50,000 in aggregate for the
review period, the Investigator shall record the source of those funds and the
documentation relied upon.

### 3.6 Disposition

**3.6.1** The Investigator shall record one of the following dispositions, together with
the rationale and the case file evidence relied upon:

| Disposition | Applied where |
|---|---|
| No further action | The activity is explained and no further review is warranted. |
| No further action — enhanced monitoring | The activity is explained but the customer's profile has changed. The customer is referred to CDD for risk rating review and placed on enhanced monitoring under AML-KYC-02 §6. |
| Refer for filing | The activity is or may be suspicious. Referred to the MLRO, who holds filing authority. |
| Refer for tuning | The alert is not substantiated by the transaction detail and the monitoring logic is referred to Tuning and Optimisation under §5.4. |
| Pending information | A request for information is outstanding. The case remains open and continues to accrue against its SLA. |

**3.6.2** A disposition of *no further action* shall be approved by a Reviewing Officer
before the case is closed in the FCCM. The approver and approval date are recorded by the
FCCM on approval.

**3.6.3** Cases shall be dispositioned within the SLA for their risk tier. Cases exceeding
SLA are reported to the Head of Investigations weekly under §5.2.

**3.6.4** Where the disposition is *refer for filing*, the Investigator shall record the
date on which the Bank first detected facts constituting a basis for filing. The filing
deadline runs thirty calendar days from that date.
